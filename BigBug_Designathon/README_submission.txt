NOTES FOR JUDGES
====================================================
- Our design focuses on "Calm Operational Clarity", providing distinct interfaces tailored to the physical working conditions of all 4 roles (Dispatcher, Loader, Driver, Store Manager).
- We have fully designed 3 degradation scenarios:
  1. Capacity Deferral & Impact (Dispatcher Desktop)
  2. Loading Shortfall (Loader Tablet)
  3. Offline & Sync Centre (Driver Mobile)
- AI Tool Disclosure and Core Tradeoff explanations are included at the end of the presentation slides.
