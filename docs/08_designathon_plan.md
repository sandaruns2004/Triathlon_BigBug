## What We Must Design

A single unified system for **four roles** in the Waypoint delivery workflow:

| Role | Device | Key Need |
|---|---|---|
| **Dispatcher** | Desktop | Plan & assign routes, handle emergencies |
| **Loader** | Tablet | Load correct goods, confirm manifest |
| **Driver** | Mobile | Navigate stops, record delivery outcomes |
| **Store Manager** | Mobile | Track incoming delivery, verify receipt |

## Key Degradation Scenarios

### Primary: "Route Collapse"
A reefer truck breaks down mid-route during the pre-dawn Fresh delivery window (03:30–08:00). Dispatcher gets alert, reassigns remaining stops to another reefer vehicle, store managers get updated ETAs.

### Secondary: "Dead Stop"
Driver arrives at outlet — closed. Driver logs, photos, dispatcher decides: defer or fail.

### Secondary: "Wrong Load"
Scanner at loading bay catches wrong item. Halt load, alert dispatcher.
