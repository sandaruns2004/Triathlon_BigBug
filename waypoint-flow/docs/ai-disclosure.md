# AI Tool Disclosure

We utilized Google Antigravity (Gemini) as our primary AI pair programmer to assist with scaffolding the application structure, generating boilerplate for Tailwind CSS, and accelerating the implementation of the Allocation Engine and Firebase integration. 

All business logic, workflow design, edge-case handling (such as offline queues and exception scenarios), and UI/UX aesthetic decisions were guided by human developers. 

The AI was specifically constrained from rewriting the core business rules without explicit instruction, ensuring the resulting system accurately reflects our intended architecture for Waypoint Flow.
